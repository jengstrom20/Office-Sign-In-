SOPPA Data Privacy Compliance

Student Online Personal Protection Act (SOPPA) Compliance Notice:

  • No PII Monetization: Student names, IDs, and reasons for visit are strictly confidential educational records. They are never sold, rented, or used for targeted marketing or profiling.
  • Encrypted Local Storage: Data entered in Kiosk mode stays strictly within district control with end-to-end local encryption protection.
  • Screen Masking & Auto-Purge: The kiosk automatically clears student input after check-in and resets after inactivity to prevent peer queue viewing.
  • Staff Access Controls: Only authorized school staff with PIN authentication can view waiting queues and historical records.
